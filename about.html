About QuickTools

QuickTools is a small collection of free tools for everyday text and number tasks. Each tool runs in your browser, so what you type stays on your device.

We add tools based on what people actually need, and we explain how each one works so you can trust the result. If something is wrong or a tool you need is missing, please tell us.
