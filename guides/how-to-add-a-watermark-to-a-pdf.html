How to Add a Watermark to a PDF

A watermark marks a document as a draft, confidential or yours. You can stamp text or a small logo on every page, or only some pages, right in your browser.

Step by step

  1. Open Add Watermark to PDF and choose your file.
  2. Type your text, or pick a preset such as CONFIDENTIAL or DRAFT. To use a logo, add a PNG or JPG image instead.
  3. Set size, opacity, angle, colour and position. A diagonal, semi-transparent mark across the centre is the classic style.
  4. Choose which pages to stamp and download the result.

Making a watermark readable but not annoying

  • Use opacity around 15 to 30 percent so the page text stays easy to read.
  • Pick a mid-tone colour; very dark marks hide text and very light ones disappear when printed.
  • For a logo, a transparent PNG looks best because it does not leave a white box.

Limits to be aware of

A watermark discourages casual copying but it is not security. Anyone with a PDF editor can remove it. If a document must stay private, do not share it at all rather than relying on a watermark.

The standard fonts used here cover English letters and numbers. For other scripts, use a logo image of your text instead.
