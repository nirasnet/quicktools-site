How to Add Page Numbers to a PDF

A long report, thesis or set of scanned pages is much easier to use when every page has a number. You can add them to an existing PDF in your browser.

Step by step

  1. Open Add Page Numbers to PDF and choose your file.
  2. Pick a format, for example "3" or "Page 3 of 12".
  3. Choose where the number goes: bottom centre is the usual choice, bottom right suits double-sided printing.
  4. Set the first number, font size and colour, then download the new PDF.

Skipping the cover page

Most documents should not number the cover. Use the skip option so the second page becomes page 1 or page 2, depending on how you want to count.

Tips for good results

  • Keep a margin from the edge so printers do not cut the number off.
  • Use a dark grey or black number on light pages. If your pages have dark backgrounds, choose a light colour.
  • Add numbers last, after you have merged and reordered pages. Merge first with Merge PDF, otherwise the numbers will repeat.

The original file is not changed. You get a new PDF, and nothing is uploaded.
