How to Turn a Photo into a Cross-Stitch or Bead Pattern

Any picture can become a stitch or bead pattern, but some pictures work far better than others. A little preparation gives a result you will enjoy finishing.

Choose a picture with clear shapes

Logos, cartoons, pets and flowers with strong contrast convert well. Busy backgrounds and soft shadows turn into noise at low resolution. Cropping tightly on your subject helps a lot.

Decide the size

Each square in the chart is one stitch or bead. On 14-count Aida cloth, a 70-stitch-wide pattern is about 12.7 cm (5 inches) wide. Fuse bead boards are usually 29 by 29 beads, so keep the width at 29 or less for a single board.

Limit the colours

Every extra colour means another thread to buy and more changes while stitching. Six to twelve colours are plenty for most designs; use fewer for a bold, graphic look.

Read the chart

Each colour has its own symbol and the legend lists how many stitches you need. Heavier lines every ten squares make it easy to count. Start at the centre and work outwards so the design stays centred on the fabric.
