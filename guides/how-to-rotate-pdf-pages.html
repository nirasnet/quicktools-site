How to Rotate PDF Pages (One Page or All of Them)

Scanned documents often come out sideways or upside down. Instead of rescanning, you can rotate the pages inside the PDF.

Step by step

  1. Open Rotate PDF and choose your file.
  2. Pick the angle: 90° clockwise, 90° anticlockwise or 180°.
  3. Choose which pages to rotate: all, odd pages only, even pages only, or a list such as 2,5-7.
  4. Download the rotated PDF.

Why odd and even pages?

If you scanned a double-sided stack in two passes, one set of pages may face the wrong way. Rotating only the odd or only the even pages fixes this in one step.

Page ranges explained

Write single pages separated by commas and ranges with a dash. For example 1,3,8-10 means pages 1, 3, 8, 9 and 10. Page numbers start at 1.

Good to know

  • Rotation changes how the page is displayed. The text and images are not re-compressed, so quality stays the same.
  • If only a few pages are wrong, use a page list rather than rotating everything.
  • You can combine this with Split PDF to pull out just the pages you need first.
