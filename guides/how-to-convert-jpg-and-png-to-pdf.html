How to Convert JPG and PNG Images to a PDF

Many forms, applications and printers want a single PDF instead of a pile of photos. You can combine JPG and PNG images into one PDF without installing anything, and without uploading your pictures.

Step by step

  1. Open the Images to PDF tool and add your pictures. Drag the thumbnails to change the page order.
  2. Choose a page size such as A4 or Letter, or let each page match its image.
  3. Pick portrait or landscape, set a margin, and use the shrink option (for example max 1600 px) if the finished PDF would otherwise be very large.
  4. Rotate any photo that came in sideways, then create the PDF.

Choosing page size and margins

Use A4 or Letter for documents you will print or submit. A small margin (about 10 mm) keeps edges from being cut off by home printers. Choose the "Same size as each image" page size for scans and photos so nothing is cropped.

Keeping the file small

Phone photos are often 3 to 8 MB each, so ten of them make a very large PDF. Shrink them first with the Image Compressor, then build the PDF. Reducing the long edge to around 2000 pixels is plenty for reading on screen and for normal printing.

Common problems

  • Photo appears sideways: use the per-image rotate button before creating the PDF.
  • Pages in the wrong order: drag the thumbnails; the PDF follows the list from top to bottom.
  • PDF too large for an upload form: compress the images first and try again.

Everything runs on your own device, so private documents such as ID scans never leave your computer or phone.
