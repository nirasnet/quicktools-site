Paper settings

What you can make

  • Square grid: maths, technical sketches, cross-stitch and bead planning, level and map design. Add bold lines every 5 or 10 squares for easy counting.
  • Dot grid: bullet journals, lettering and sketching with less visual noise than lines.
  • Cross marks: a light grid of small plus signs for drawing and layout.
  • Lined: note paper at the spacing you prefer (7 mm is close to standard college ruled).
  • Isometric dots: for 3D sketches, product drawings and game art.

Printing tips

In the print dialog choose Actual size or 100%, not "fit to page", so a 5 mm grid really measures 5 mm. Printers cannot reach the very edge of the page, so keep a margin of at least 5 mm. Use a light grey if you plan to write over the top, and a darker colour with the bold weight for rulers and technical drawings.

How it works

The sheet is drawn as a vector PDF, so lines stay sharp at any zoom and the file is tiny. It is created in your browser and nothing is uploaded.
