Printable Graph & Dot Paper

Make printable grid, dot-grid or lined paper as a PDF in A4, A5 or Letter, with the spacing you choose.

🔒 Runs in your browser: nothing is uploaded

What you can make

  • Square grid: maths homework, technical sketches, cross-stitch and bead planning, level and map design.
  • Dot grid: bullet journals, lettering practice and sketching, with less visual noise than lines.
  • Lined: note paper with the line spacing you prefer (7 mm is close to standard college ruled).

Printing tips

In the print dialog choose Actual size or 100%, not "fit to page", so a 5 mm grid really measures 5 mm. Printers cannot reach the very edge of the page, so keep a margin of at least 5 mm. Choose a light grey colour if you plan to write or draw over the top; pick a darker one for rulers and technical drawings.

How it works

The sheet is drawn as a vector PDF, so lines stay sharp at any zoom and the file is tiny. It is created in your browser, and nothing is uploaded.
