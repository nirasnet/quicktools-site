Age Calculator

Find your exact age in years, months and days, and the days until your next birthday.

🔒
Private by design: this tool runs in your browser, not on our server. Your files and text are processed on your own device and are never uploaded. How we keep your data safe

How age is calculated

Your age is the number of full years, then full months, then remaining days between your date of birth and today. When the day of the month hasn't been reached yet, a month is borrowed from the month before, which is why results can differ by a day from simple "divide by 365" estimates.

Leap years

Total days lived uses real calendar days, so leap days are included. People born on 29 February celebrate on 28 February or 1 March in non-leap years, depending on local custom and law. This tool counts the next birthday on 1 March.
