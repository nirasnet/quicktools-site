Loan & EMI Calculator

Estimate your monthly loan payment, total interest and total cost from amount, rate and term.

How the payment is calculated

For a standard amortising loan the monthly payment is P × r × (1+r)^n ÷ ((1+r)^n − 1), where P is the amount borrowed, r is the monthly rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n is the number of monthly payments. If the rate is 0%, the payment is simply P ÷ n.

What it does not include

Fees, insurance, taxes and variable rates are not included, and lenders may round differently or use daily interest. Treat the result as a close estimate and check the lender's official figures before committing.

Ways to pay less interest

  • Choose a shorter term: the payment is higher but total interest drops sharply.
  • Make extra payments toward principal when your loan allows it without penalty.
  • Compare offers by total paid, not only by monthly payment.
