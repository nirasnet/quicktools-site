Unit Converter

Convert length, weight and temperature between metric and imperial units.

How the conversion works

Length and weight units are converted through a common base unit (metres and kilograms) using exact definitions, for example 1 inch = 0.0254 m and 1 pound = 0.45359237 kg. Temperature uses formulas instead of a single factor because the scales have different zero points.

Temperature formulas

  • °F = °C × 9/5 + 32
  • °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9
  • K = °C + 273.15

Handy reference points

Water freezes at 0 °C (32 °F) and boils at 100 °C (212 °F) at sea level. A mile is about 1.609 kilometres, and a kilogram is about 2.205 pounds.

Rounding

Results are shown to about eight significant digits, so tiny differences you may see in other tools come from rounding, not from different definitions.
