Tip & Bill Split Calculator

Work out the tip, the total bill and each person's share when splitting a bill.

How it is calculated

Tip = bill × tip percentage ÷ 100. Total = bill + tip. Each person pays the total divided by the number of people. Amounts are rounded to two decimals, so shares may differ from the total by a cent.

Typical tipping norms

Customs vary widely. In the United States 15% to 20% of the pre-tax bill is common at restaurants. In many European countries service is included or a small round-up is usual, and in parts of Asia tipping is not expected. Check local custom when you travel.

Splitting fairly

This tool splits evenly. If people ordered very different amounts, work out each person's own items first and apply the tip percentage to each.
