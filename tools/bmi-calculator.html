BMI Calculator

Calculate body mass index from height and weight in metric or imperial units.

🔒
Private by design: this tool runs in your browser, not on our server. Your files and text are processed on your own device and are never uploaded. How we keep your data safe

The formula

BMI = weight in kilograms ÷ (height in metres)². In imperial units it is 703 × weight in pounds ÷ (height in inches)². For example, 70 kg at 1.70 m gives 70 ÷ 2.89 = 24.2.

Standard adult ranges

  • Below 18.5: underweight range
  • 18.5 to 24.9: healthy weight range
  • 25 to 29.9: overweight range
  • 30 and above: obesity range

What BMI can't tell you

BMI uses only height and weight. It does not separate muscle from fat, and it does not account for age, sex, or where body fat is carried. Athletes can have a high BMI with low body fat. Ranges also differ for children and teenagers, who use age-based percentile charts.

Important

This tool is for general information and is not medical advice. Talk to a doctor or dietitian about your weight and health.
