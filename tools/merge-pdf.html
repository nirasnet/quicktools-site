Merge PDF

Join several PDFs into one. Drag to reorder, and choose which pages to take from each file.

🔒
Private by design: this tool runs in your browser, not on our server. Your files and text are processed on your own device and are never uploaded. How we keep your data safe

    Output

    Tip: type pages next to a file to take only part of it, for example 1-3, 7. Leave it empty to use every page.

    How to merge PDF files

    1. Add the PDFs you want to combine. You can add more at any time.
    2. Drag the ⠿ handle to set the order. To use only part of a file, type its pages in the box next to it, such as 1-3, 7.
    3. Press Merge PDFs. A single file downloads to your device.

    What is kept

    Pages are copied as they are, including text, images and page sizes. Bookmarks, form fields and links that point to other pages inside the original files may not survive a merge, because the page numbers change.

    Password-protected files

    Encrypted PDFs cannot be read here. Remove the password in the program that created the file, then add it again.

    Privacy and size

    Merging happens on your computer, so confidential contracts or invoices are never sent to any server. Very large files (hundreds of MB) may be slow because the browser has to hold them in memory.
