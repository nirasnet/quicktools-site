Merge PDF

Join several PDF files into one document in the order you choose. Runs in your browser.

🔒 Runs in your browser: nothing is uploaded

    How to merge PDF files

    1. Add the PDFs you want to combine. You can add more than once.
    2. Move files up or down so they are in the final order, and remove any you added by mistake.
    3. Press Merge PDFs. A single merged.pdf downloads to your device.

    What is kept

    Pages are copied as they are, including text, images and page sizes. Bookmarks, form fields and links that point to other pages inside the original files may not survive a merge, because the page numbers change.

    Password-protected files

    Encrypted PDFs cannot be read here. Remove the password in the program that created the file, then add it again.

    Privacy and size

    Merging happens on your computer, so confidential contracts or invoices are not sent to any server. Very large files (hundreds of MB) may be slow because the browser has to hold them in memory.
