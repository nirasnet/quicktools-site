How to make a pattern

  1. Choose a picture. Simple images with clear shapes work best: logos, pets, flowers, portraits with strong contrast.
  2. Set the width in stitches and the number of colours you are willing to buy. Fewer colours look more graphic and are easier to stitch.
  3. Pick your fabric count to see the finished size in inches and centimetres. A 14-count Aida has 14 stitches per inch.
  4. Use brightness and contrast to bring out detail, or tick Leave light background empty to skip stitching the white areas.
  5. Click a colour in the legend to highlight every square that uses it, then download the chart.

Uses beyond cross-stitch

The same chart works for Perler or Hama fuse beads, pixel art, tapestry crochet, knitting colourwork, mosaics, quilt blocks and brick art. Each square is one unit of your craft.

Reading the chart

Heavier grid lines appear every 5 or 10 squares to help you count and find the centre. Start from the middle and work outwards to keep the design centred. Colours are matched by clustering, so the hex codes are close matches, not thread-brand numbers: compare against a thread or bead chart in good light before buying.
