Split & Extract PDF Pages

Extract pages into one PDF, or split a PDF into many files by ranges, every N pages or one page each (ZIP).

🔒
Private by design: this tool runs in your browser, not on our server. Your files and text are processed on your own device and are never uploaded. How we keep your data safe

Split options

Four ways to split

  • Extract pages: type pages and ranges such as 1-3, 5, 8- to build one new PDF. Pages come out in the order you type them, so 3, 1 puts page 3 first.
  • Split by ranges: separate groups with semicolons, for example 1-3; 4-6; 7-, to make several PDFs at once.
  • Every N pages: cut a long document into equal parts, for example a 100-page scan into 10-page files.
  • One PDF per page: every page becomes its own file.

When more than one file is made, they download together as a single ZIP.

Common uses

  • Send only the relevant pages of a long contract or report.
  • Remove a cover page or blank page by listing everything else.
  • Break a scanned batch of invoices into one file per invoice.

Privacy

The original file is never uploaded. New PDFs and the ZIP are created in your browser and saved to your downloads folder.
