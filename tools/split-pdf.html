Split & Extract PDF Pages

Pull specific pages or ranges out of a PDF into a new file, for example 1-3, 5, 8-.

🔒 Runs in your browser: nothing is uploaded

How to choose pages

Type page numbers and ranges separated by commas. Examples: 1-3 keeps pages 1 to 3; 2, 4, 6 keeps those three pages; 5- keeps page 5 to the end; -3 keeps the first three pages. Pages appear in the new file in the order you type them, so 3, 1 puts page 3 first.

Common uses

  • Send only the relevant pages of a long contract or report.
  • Remove a cover page or blank page by listing everything else.
  • Re-order pages by typing them in a new sequence.

Privacy

The original file is never uploaded. The new PDF is created in your browser and saved to your downloads folder.
