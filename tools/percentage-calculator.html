What is X% of Y?

X is what % of Y?

Percentage change from X to Y

The three formulas

  • X% of Y = X ÷ 100 × Y. Example: 15% of 80 = 12.
  • X is what % of Y = X ÷ Y × 100. Example: 30 is 37.5% of 80.
  • Percentage change = (new − old) ÷ |old| × 100. Example: 50 to 65 is +30%.

Percentage points vs percent

If a rate moves from 10% to 12%, it rose by 2 percentage points, but by 20 percent in relative terms. Use the percentage change box for relative change.

Everyday examples

Discounts (a 25% sale on a 40 item saves 10), tips, exam scores, and growth in sales or traffic from one month to the next.

Percentage change from zero

If the starting value is 0, percentage change is undefined, so the calculator leaves the result blank rather than show a misleading number.
