Word Counter

Count words, characters, sentences and reading time. See your most-used words and how close you are to common length limits.

How the word counter works

Paste any text and the counts update as you type. Words are runs of non-space characters, so "well-known" counts as one word. Sentences are counted by terminal punctuation (. ! ?), and paragraphs are blocks separated by a blank line.

Reading and speaking time

Reading time assumes about 200 words per minute, a common average for adults reading online. Speaking time assumes about 130 words per minute, a comfortable pace for a talk or video script.

Most used words

The list shows the words you repeat most, ignoring very common words such as "the" and "and". It helps you spot overused words and check keyword density in an article.

Length limits

The last row shows how many characters you have left for common limits: an X (Twitter) post at 280, a search-result meta description at about 160, a single SMS at 160 and a page title tag at about 60. A negative number means you are over.

Everything runs in your browser. Your text is never uploaded or stored.
