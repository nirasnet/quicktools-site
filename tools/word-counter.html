Word Counter

Count words, characters, sentences and reading time instantly.

🔒 Runs in your browser: nothing is uploaded

How the word counter works

Paste any text and the counts update as you type. Words are runs of non-space characters, so "well-known" counts as one word. Sentences are counted by terminal punctuation (. ! ?), and paragraphs are blocks separated by a blank line.

Reading time

Reading time assumes about 200 words per minute, a common average for adults reading online. Technical text is usually slower; light fiction is faster.

Common uses

  • Keeping essays and assignments inside a word limit.
  • Checking meta descriptions (about 155 characters) and social posts (X/Twitter allows 280).
  • Estimating how long a blog post or speech will take.

Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Your text is never uploaded or stored.
