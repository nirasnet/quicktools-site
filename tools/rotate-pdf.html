Rotation

When you need it

Scanned documents and phone photos often come out sideways or upside down. Rotating them here fixes the page orientation for good, so the file opens the right way up in every viewer, not just in your own.

Choosing pages

Rotate all pages, only the odd or even ones (useful when a double-sided scan has every second sheet upside down), or type specific pages such as 2, 4-6.

Good to know

  • Rotation is saved as a page setting, so the text, images and quality are untouched.
  • Rotating twice adds up: pressing the button again turns the same pages another step.
  • Password-protected PDFs must be unlocked in the program that created them first.

The work happens in your browser and the file is never uploaded.
