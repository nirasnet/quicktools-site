Add Watermark to PDF

Stamp text such as CONFIDENTIAL or DRAFT across every page of a PDF. Choose size, colour, opacity and angle.

🔒 Runs in your browser: nothing is uploaded

How to watermark a PDF

  1. Drop in your PDF.
  2. Type the text, for example CONFIDENTIAL, DRAFT or your name.
  3. Pick size, colour, opacity and angle, then press the button. The stamped copy downloads and your original file is not changed.

Settings that work well

  • Opacity 15 to 30% keeps the page readable while the mark stays visible.
  • Diagonal is the classic look and is hard to crop out.
  • Tiled repeats the text across the page, which protects documents you share with clients.

Limits

The text uses the built-in Helvetica Bold font, which covers English letters, numbers and common symbols. Thai, Chinese, Arabic and emoji are not supported in the watermark text. A watermark deters casual reuse but is not security: anyone with editing software can remove it, so do not rely on it to protect secrets.

Your file is processed in your browser and is never uploaded.
