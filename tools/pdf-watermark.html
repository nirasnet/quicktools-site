Watermark design

How to watermark a PDF

  1. Drop in your PDF.
  2. Pick a ready-made word such as CONFIDENTIAL or DRAFT, or type your own. You can also choose a logo image instead.
  3. Adjust font, size, colour, opacity, angle and position, then press the button. Your original file is not changed.

Settings that work well

  • Opacity 15 to 30% keeps the page readable while the mark stays visible.
  • Diagonal (45°) is the classic look and is hard to crop out.
  • Tiled repeats the mark across the page, which protects documents you share with clients.
  • Single mark in a corner suits a small company name or "Page for review".
  • Use the pages box to mark only some pages, for example 1 for the cover.

Limits

Text uses the built-in PDF fonts, which cover English letters, numbers and common symbols. For Thai, Chinese, Arabic or emoji, use the logo option with a PNG of your text. A watermark deters casual reuse but is not security: anyone with editing software can remove it.

Your file and logo are processed in your browser and are never uploaded.
