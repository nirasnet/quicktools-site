Colour Palette from Image

Pull the main colours out of any photo and copy their hex codes. Perfect for design, painting and crafts.

🔒 Runs in your browser: nothing is uploaded

How it works

The image is shrunk to a small copy and its pixels are grouped into the number of colour clusters you choose, using a method called k-means clustering. Each swatch is the average colour of one cluster, and the percentage shows how much of the picture it covers. Colours are listed from most to least common.

What to use it for

  • Build a website or slide palette from a photo or logo.
  • Choose yarn, thread, paint or fabric colours that match a reference picture.
  • Pick matching colours for a room or outfit.

Tips

Use fewer colours (3 to 5) for a clean brand palette and more (8 to 10) to capture the full range of a painting. Very small accent colours can be absorbed into a larger group, so crop the picture to the area you care about for a more exact result. The image never leaves your device.
