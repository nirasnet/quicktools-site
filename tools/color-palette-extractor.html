Two ways to get colours

Main colours: the picture is shrunk and its pixels are grouped into the number of colour clusters you choose (k-means clustering). Each swatch is the average colour of one group, and the percentage shows how much of the picture it covers. Click the picture to pick the exact colour of any single pixel, for example the red of one flower.

Formats and export

Switch between HEX, RGB and HSL, click any swatch to copy it, or copy the whole palette as CSS variables or JSON to paste straight into a stylesheet or design tool.

What to use it for

  • Build a website or slide palette from a photo or logo.
  • Choose yarn, thread, paint or fabric colours that match a reference picture.
  • Match colours for a room, outfit or art project.

Use fewer colours (3 to 5) for a clean brand palette and more (8 to 12) for a painting. Your image never leaves your device.
