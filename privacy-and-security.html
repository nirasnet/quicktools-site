How Your Data Stays Safe

The short version: every tool on QuickTools runs inside your own web browser. Your PDFs, images and text are read by your device, processed by your device, and saved by your device. They are never uploaded to our servers, because our tools have no server to upload to.

What "runs in your browser" means

When you open a tool, your browser downloads the page and a small amount of code. After that, all the work happens on your computer or phone: merging a PDF, shrinking a photo, drawing a QR code. The result is created in your browser's memory and saved straight to your downloads folder. There is no step where your file is sent anywhere.

Check it yourself in one minute

  1. Offline test. Open any tool, then turn off Wi-Fi or switch to airplane mode. Add a file and use the tool. It still works, which would be impossible if it needed a server.
  2. Network test. Press F12 (or right-click and choose Inspect), open the Network tab and use a tool. You will see no requests carrying your file. The only requests are the page files that loaded before you started.
  3. Security-rule test. View the page source and look at the Content-Security-Policy line at the top. It contains connect-src 'none', an instruction that makes the browser block the page from making any network request. Even a coding mistake could not send your data out.

No third-party code on tool pages

The PDF library (pdf-lib) and QR library (qrcode-generator) are open-source and are hosted on this website itself, not loaded from another company's server. Tool pages use no analytics or tracking scripts that could see your files.

What is saved on your device

To save you time, some tools remember your option choices (for example page size or image quality) in your browser's local storage. This stays on your device, is never sent to us, and never includes file contents, text you typed, names or passwords. Use the "Reset options" button on a tool page, or clear your browser's site data, to remove it.

What we and others can see

  • Hosting. This site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host it may keep standard access logs, such as your IP address and the page you opened. These logs never include your files, because your files never leave your device.
  • Ads. We do not show ads yet. If we do in the future, Google AdSense scripts may be added to pages and may use cookies, as described in our Privacy Policy. Ad scripts do not receive your files, but they are third-party code, and this page and the policy will be updated before ads go live.
  • Contact. If you email us, we see your email address and your message.

Good habits

  • Download your result and close the tab when you finish; nothing is kept.
  • On a shared computer, use a private or guest window.
  • Only use tools on sites you trust. Keep your browser up to date.
