Hand-in-hand, part 1: clean up your Android TV with no ads and no coding

Step-by-step guide to remove ads and junk apps from an Android TV or Google TV with a free AI assistant. No root, and you can restore it.

By 9M4ck · Beginner

What you will get

  • Clears ads and junk apps from Android TV / Google TV
  • No root needed, and no apps are deleted
  • You can undo it with a restore command

After the first post, a lot of people asked me, "Can you walk me through it?" Here it is. Follow the photos step by step (taken on our real TV at home, with the Thai menus).

Get 3 things ready

  • An Android TV / Google TV (TCL, Sony, Xiaomi, Chromecast and so on)
  • A Mac or Windows computer on the same Wi-Fi as the TV
  • One AI assistant on the computer: Codex (free with ChatGPT), Antigravity (free from Google) or Claude Code
No AI on your computer yet? Read Start from zero: install an AI assistant on your computer

Steps

  1. Turn on developer mode (on the TV, with the remote) Settings › System › About › scroll to "Android TV OS build" and press OK 7 times in a row
  2. Turn on USB debugging Go back to System › "Developer options" › turn on "USB debugging"
  3. Paste the prompt into the AI chat (copy it below) and change only the TV model
  4. Allow it on the TV The TV pops up a question. Tick "Always allow from this computer" and press Allow

Then sit back while the AI turns off the ads, turns off the junk apps, and sets up a new home screen.

No apps are deleted. If you want things back the way they were, tell the AI "restore my TV"

The prompt

Talk to me in Thai. Clone https://github.com/Gathin23/cleanyourtv and work inside that folder.
Open its PROMPT.md and do everything between the two --- lines, exactly as if I had pasted it to you.
My TV model is: ____ (if I left this blank, read the model from the TV once you are connected).

All the steps, plus prompt 2 (kids mode, viewing-time rules, iPhone app), are on one page at github.com/nirasnet/peach-tv. You can also read the article on kids mode for the TV

Safety warning. Developer mode and USB debugging let a computer control the TV at a deep level. Turn USB debugging off again when you're done, and use this only on your own TV.
