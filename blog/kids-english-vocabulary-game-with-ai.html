I Made an English Word Game for My Kid With AI in Under Two Minutes

One request, one file. A word game that runs in Safari on an iPad, with big buttons, spoken words and stars.

9MBy 9M4ck
5 min read · Beginner · Updated 22 Nov 2026

What you will get

  • One prompt makes a game as a single file
  • Open it by double-clicking, or in Safari on an iPad
  • Add your own categories by chatting with the AI
On this pageWhat is in the gameHow I made itThe prompt

I wanted a simple English word game for my kid, so I tried asking an AI. I typed one request, waited 1 minute 25 seconds, and got one file. Double-click, and it plays. I played all 10 questions myself to check it. The screenshots below are from that game.

What is in the game

3 categoriesanimals, fruit, colours
Spoken wordstap and it reads the English word
Kind feedbackstars for right, "try again" for wrong
A rewardtrophy and confetti after 10

How I made it

  1. Open your AI assistant (Codex, Antigravity or Claude Code all work). Not installed yet? Start here.
  2. Copy the prompt below and paste it in.
  3. Wait a moment. You get a file named index.html.
  4. Double-click it. On an iPad, open it in Safari.

The prompt

Make an English vocabulary guessing game for my 6-year-old that works on an iPad.
It must be a single index.html file that opens in Safari without installing anything.
- Show a large emoji and let the child tap one of 3 answer buttons. Big buttons that are easy to tap.
- Tapping an answer reads the English word aloud.
- A correct answer shows a bouncing star and a praise sound. A wrong answer says try again, no scolding.
- Keep score. After 10 questions show a "well done" screen.
- Categories to choose from: animals, fruit, colours.
- Bright colours, large text, with a little Thai labelling.

My original prompt was in Thai. This is the same request in English. Change "6-year-old" and the categories to suit your child.

Want more? Keep chatting with the AI. Ask for numbers or vehicles, or a different age. Play it yourself before you hand it to your child.
