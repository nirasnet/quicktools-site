What the app can do

  • Tick a chore as done and get a star right away, with a pop and a sound
  • A Monday-to-Sunday chart
  • Finish every chore for the day and get fireworks and a compliment
  • Trade stars for rewards, such as "Ice cream, 10 stars"
  • Add your own chores and rewards
  • Saved on the device, so it is still there after you close and reopen it

Just 3 steps

  1. Open gemini.google.com, tap the + button and choose Canvas
  2. Copy the prompt below, paste it and send (it takes under a minute)
  3. Tap Preview and you can play with it right away

Want an icon on your iPhone? See episode 1 (Safari → ••• More → Share → Add to Home Screen)

The prompt

Please make a chore star-chart app for my kids. It should be a single web page file that works on an iPhone.
- Show a list of chores to choose from, such as tidying toys, folding clothes, watering plants, washing dishes, feeding the cat. Each chore has a big emoji.
- A Monday-to-Sunday chart. Tap to tick a chore as done and get a star, with the star popping up and a ding sound.
- I can add chores myself and set how many stars each chore is worth.
- Rewards that stars can be traded for, such as "Ice cream, 10 stars" and "30 minutes of games, 15 stars". When there are enough stars, a tap redeems the reward.
- When all chores for that day are done, show a compliment with fireworks.
- Save data on the device so it is still there after closing and reopening. Add a button to start a new week.
- The app should be in Thai, with big text and bright colors that suit children.
Made with AI. The app code, the video and the voice-over for this episode were made with AI tools.
