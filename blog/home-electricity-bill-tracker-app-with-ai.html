Hands-On, Part 4: A Home Electricity Bill Tracker Made With AI, No Sign-Up, No Coding

I tried it for real again. I typed to the AI once, waited 1 minute, and got one file for an app. You can open it on a phone or a computer.

(The numbers in the clip are sample data. The screens are the real app the AI made.)

What it does

  • Enter the month, units used and amount from your bill
  • A bar chart compares every month
  • Any month up more than 20% gets a red bar and a reminder to check your appliances
  • Average price per unit
  • Data is stored on your own device and is still there when you close and reopen it
  1. Open your AI app (Codex / Antigravity / Claude Code; if you don't have one, see part 0)
  2. Copy the prompt below and paste it in
  3. You get a file called index.html. Open it and start entering your bills

If you want to add water, internet, or reminders through LINE, just keep telling the AI.

The prompt

Please make a monthly home electricity bill tracker website. It should work on a phone, as a single index.html file that opens right away with no sign-up.
- I enter the month, the number of electricity units and the amount, then tap Save
- Show a bar chart of the electricity cost for each month
- Show whether the latest month went up or down, in percent, compared with the previous month. If it went up a lot, warn me in red
- Calculate the average price per unit
- Keep the data on the device so it is still there after closing and reopening, with a button to delete an entry
- The app should be in Thai, with big text that is easy to read and comfortable colors

Whose electricity bill jumped this month? Feel free to share.
