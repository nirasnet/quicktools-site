Hands-on App Episode 4: Send Transfer Slips to AI and Get a Monthly Spending Summary

Slips piling up on your phone, and you can't tell where the money went this month? Send photos of the slips to an AI and let it read them for you.

I'm using just Gemini on the web (free), same as before. I don't write a single line of code myself.

(The slips in the clip are sample slips, not real transactions.)

What the app can do

  • A big total for the whole month at the top
  • A pie chart by category; tap a category to see only the items in it
  • Shows which category you spent the most on, with a tip
  • For bills without a slip, you can add, edit or delete items yourself
  • Saved on the device, so it is still there after you close and reopen it

Just 3 steps

  1. Open gemini.google.com, tap the + button → Upload files, choose the slip photos, then tap + again and choose Canvas
  2. Copy the prompt below, paste it and send (it takes under a minute)
  3. Tap Preview and you can see the summary right away

Want an icon on your iPhone? See episode 1 (Safari → ••• More → Share → Add to Home Screen)

Slips show your name and account number. If you don't want the AI to see them, crop that part out first. The date, shop and amount are enough.

  • The AI can misread things. Check the total against your slips again.

The prompt

These are my household's transfer slips for this month. Please read every one and make an app that summarizes this month's spending. It should be a single web page file that works on an iPhone.
- Put the items from every slip into the app: date, who was paid, amount, category (food, housing, transport, kids, household items)
- A big total for the whole month at the top
- A pie chart by category. Tapping a category shows the items in that category.
- Say which category cost the most, with one short tip
- I can add, edit and delete items myself. Save data on the device so it is still there after closing and reopening.
- The app should be in Thai, with big text that is easy to read and easy on the eyes.
