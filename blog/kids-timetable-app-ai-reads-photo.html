Hands-On App Building, Episode 9: A Kid's Timetable App That Reads a Photo of the Schedule

Photograph your child's school timetable and let Gemini build an app that shows tomorrow's classes and what to pack, free and with no coding.

By 9M4ck · Beginner

What you will get

  • The home screen shows today's classes by period and which period it is now
  • Tap another day, or tap the Tomorrow button
  • A checklist of things to pack each day, ticked off once they are in the bag
On this pageWhat the app can doThe prompt

What does my child have tomorrow, and what do they need to pack? Take a photo of the timetable, let AI read it, and have it build the app for you.

It is still just Gemini on the web (free). I do not write a single line of code.

Try the finished appThe code the AI wrote (full file)Sample timetable photo (drawn by me, not a real timetable)

What the app can do

  • The home screen shows today's classes by period and which period it is now
  • Tap another day, or tap the "Tomorrow" button
  • A list of things to pack each day, ticked off once they are in the bag
  • Reminder notes, such as handing in homework or lunch money, which you can edit or delete
  • Copy tomorrow's schedule and send it to the family on LINE
  • Saved on your device, still there after you close and reopen it

There are only 3 things to do.

  1. Open gemini.google.com, press the + button, attach the photo of the timetable, and choose Canvas
  2. Copy the prompt below, paste it, and send it (wait about 1 minute)
  3. Press Preview and it is ready to use

Want an icon on your phone: see the special episode (iPhone: Safari → Share button → Add to Home Screen · Android: Chrome → ⋮ → Install app)

AI can misread a timetable from a photo. Always check it against the school's real timetable before you use it.

(The timetable in the clip is a sample I drew myself. It is not from any school.)

The prompt

This is a photo of my child's school timetable. Please read it and build a timetable app, as a single-file web page that works on iPhone.
- Put the timetable for every day from Monday to Friday from the photo into the app
- The home screen shows "What's on today" in big text, in order of period, and shows which period it is right now
- I can tap another day to look at it, and there is a "Tomorrow" button
- I can add a list of things to pack for each day, such as PE clothes and notebooks, and tick them off once they are in the bag
- I can add reminder notes per day, such as handing in homework or lunch money, and edit or delete them
- A "Copy tomorrow's schedule" button to send to the family
- Save the data on the device so it is still there after closing and reopening
- Thai language, large text, easy to read, soft colors
