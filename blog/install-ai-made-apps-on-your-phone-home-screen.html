Hands-on App Special: Put AI-Made Apps on Your Phone as Full-Screen Apps with Icons

How to add the AI-made web apps to your phone's home screen with an icon and full-screen mode, free, with no extra accounts to sign up for.

By 9M4ck · Beginner

What you will get

  • Brushing teeth: https://jabmueai.github.io/jabmue-apps/brush/
  • Piggy bank: https://jabmueai.github.io/jabmue-apps/piggy/
  • Chores: https://jabmueai.github.io/jabmue-apps/chores/
On this pageAll the apps made in the clips

Way 1: use the Jabmue AI apps directly.

Every app now has an icon. It also opens without internet, after the first open while online. The data stays on your own device and is not sent anywhere.

iPhone: open the link in Safari → Share button → Add to Home Screen → Add

Way 2: an app you made yourself in Gemini.

Tap Share → copy the link → open it on your phone → Add to Home Screen, the same way (the icon will be the Gemini logo).

Can you publish on the Play Store or App Store? Yes, but there is a developer fee (Google Play is $25 once, and you need to find people to test before release. App Store is $99 a year, and you need a Mac). For use at home, the 2 ways above are enough.

These apps are still web pages, so there are no notifications once the app is closed (the medicine reminder app has to stay open).

(The home screen and the Android menu in the clip are illustrations. The Safari screen is real, from episode 1.)
