I'm a dad who likes trying new tech, especially AI that you can tell what to do in plain language and that then makes something you can really use.

Lately I've had AI build a kids mode for our TV, an app that controls the TV from my phone, a video, and a voice-over. This post was written with AI's help too, and I haven't written a single line of code myself.

What I found is that ordinary people can make things like this now. You just need someone to hold your hand the first time.

So I opened this page to share what I like and have actually tried, so you can follow along.

What you'll find on this page

  • Things that are useful at home: the TV, games for the kids, helper bots

About the author

Hi, I'm 9M4ck, a dad who loves trying new tech, especially AI that you can talk to in plain words and that builds something useful. I don't write code myself. I started Jubmue AI (จับมือ AI) because friends kept asking me to walk them through the things I made at home, like the family TV setup.

The photos and screenshots here are from my own TV, phone and projects. The videos and voice-overs on my Facebook page are made with AI tools. This blog adapts the written steps from my posts, so you can follow along at your own pace.
