Our TV now has a kids mode, and I didn't write a single line of code

I used AI to turn an Android TV into a two-mode TV with a kids mode and a parent mode, plus a phone app to control it.

By 9M4ck · Beginner

  • Parent mode: switch from the phone to get all apps back; turning the TV off returns it to Kid mode

You may have seen CleanYourTV around lately. It cleans up the Android TV home screen, with no ads and no root needed.

I tried it at home too. The home screen got a lot cleaner. But with a child in the house, the next question came up by itself: "How do I make sure my kid only watches what they should, without a parent standing guard by the TV?"

So I held AI's hand again and turned it into a holiday toy: one TV, two modes

  • Kid mode: the home screen shows only the apps I pick, and opening anything else sends it back home
  • Parent mode: switch from the phone to get all apps back; turning the TV off returns it to Kid mode (so I can't forget)

Plus a phone app to control the TV

  • See whether the TV is on, which app is open, and grab a screenshot
  • Lock the screen with a message: "Time to rest your eyes"
  • Set several viewing windows per day plus a daily hour limit; the TV enforces them itself, even if the internet drops
  • Lock the home TV from anywhere when I'm out

All I did was type to the AI in everyday language, then check its work. The clips and photos are real screens from our TV at home.
