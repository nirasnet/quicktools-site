Hands-On App Building, Episode 8: A Blood Pressure Log That Reads Your Monitor Photo

Photograph your blood pressure monitor and let Gemini read the numbers and build a log app with a chart, free and with no coding.

By 9M4ck · Beginner

What you will get

  • The latest reading is shown big, with a color for the level: green, yellow or red
  • A chart of past systolic and diastolic readings
  • Add new readings with a photo of the monitor screen, and edit or delete them
On this pageWhat the app can doThe prompt

Do you write down your blood pressure on paper and then lose it every time? Let AI read the numbers from a photo of the monitor screen and build a log with a chart.

It is still just Gemini on the web (free). I do not write a single line of code.

Try the finished appThe code the AI wrote (full file)5 sample monitor screen photos (drawn by me, not real readings)

What the app can do

  • The latest reading is shown big, with a color for the level: green, yellow or red
  • A chart of past systolic and diastolic readings
  • Add a new reading, attach a photo of the monitor screen to look at later, edit or delete it
  • A button to copy a summary to send to the doctor or someone at home
  • Saved on your device, still there after you close and reopen it

There are only 3 things to do.

  1. Open gemini.google.com, press the + button, attach the photos of the monitor screen, and choose Canvas
  2. Copy the prompt below, paste it, and send it (wait about 1 minute)
  3. Press Preview and it is ready to use

Want an icon on your phone: see the special episode (iPhone: Safari → Share button → Add to Home Screen · Android: Chrome → ⋮ → Install app)

This app only helps you keep a record. It is not a diagnosis. If your readings stay high or you feel unwell, talk to a doctor.

(The photos and numbers in the clip are samples, not anyone's real readings · AI can misread numbers from a photo, so always check against the monitor screen first)

The prompt

These are photos of my blood pressure monitor screen from 5 days. Please read the numbers in every photo and build a blood pressure log app, as a single-file web page that works on iPhone.
- Put the values from all 5 photos into the app: date, time, systolic, diastolic, pulse
- Show the latest reading big at the top, with a color for the level (normal: green / slightly high: yellow / high: red)
- A line chart of past systolic and diastolic readings
- I can add new readings, attach a photo of the monitor screen, edit them and delete them
- A "Copy summary" button to send to the doctor or someone at home
- Save the data on the device so it is still there after closing and reopening
- A small note saying this app is not a diagnosis and to see a doctor
- Thai language, large text, easy to read, soft colors
