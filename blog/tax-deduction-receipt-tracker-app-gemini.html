Make an App, Part 6: A Tax Deduction Receipt Tracker, Made Free With AI

A free app for keeping tax deduction receipts, with yearly totals by category, photo attachments and a copyable summary for tax filing.

By 9M4ck · Beginner

What you will get

  • A big yearly total with cards by category: life insurance, health insurance, parents' health insurance, donations, home loan interest, other
  • Tap a category to see its receipts
  • Add a new receipt with a photo attached; edit or delete anytime
On this pageWhat the app can doThe prompt

Year-end is near. Where are all your insurance receipts, donation receipts and home loan interest papers? Make an app that keeps them in one place and adds up the totals.

I'm using just Gemini on the web (free), same as before. I don't write a single line of code myself.

Try the finished appThe code the AI wrote (full file)

What the app can do

  • A big yearly total, with cards by category: life insurance, health insurance, parents' health insurance, donations, home loan interest, other
  • Tap a category to see the receipts in it
  • Add a new receipt, take a photo or pick one to attach, and edit or delete it
  • A checklist of the original documents to keep for tax filing
  • A copy-summary button that gives the total for each category, to use when filling in the tax return
  • Saved on the device, so it is still there after you close and reopen it

Just 3 steps

  1. Open gemini.google.com, tap the + button and choose Canvas
  2. Copy the prompt below, paste it, change the amounts to your own receipts, then send (it takes about 1 minute)
  3. Tap Preview and you can use it right away

Want an icon on your phone? See the special episode (iPhone: Safari → Share button → Add to Home Screen · Android: Chrome → ⋮ → Install app)

The app only helps add up the totals. It does not calculate tax and is not tax advice. Check the deduction rights and limits with the Revenue Department every year, and keep the original receipts too.

(The receipts in the clip are samples. The company names and numbers are made up.)

The prompt

Please make an app for keeping my household's tax deduction receipts. It should be a single web page file that works on an iPhone.
- Put in 5 sample receipts: health insurance 12,000 (15 Jan), life insurance 18,500 (3 Mar), donation 2,000 (12 May), parents' health insurance 9,800 (20 Jul), home loan interest 42,300 (31 Oct)
- Categories: life insurance, health insurance, parents' health insurance, donations, home loan interest, other
- A big yearly total at the top, and a colored card for each category. Tapping a category shows its receipts.
- I can add new receipts, take a photo or pick a photo of the receipt to attach, and edit and delete them.
- A checklist of documents to keep for tax filing, which I can tick.
- A "Copy summary" button that gives a text summary of the total for each category, to use when filling in the tax return.
- Save data on the device so it is still there after closing and reopening.
- Don't calculate deduction limits. Add a small note telling me to check the latest rights with the Revenue Department.
- The app should be in Thai, with big text that is easy to read and easy on the eyes.
